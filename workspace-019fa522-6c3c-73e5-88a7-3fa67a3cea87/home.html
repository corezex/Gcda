FAQs

Common questions about counselling and plans

A few answers to help you understand how GCDA works before you book your consultation.

How long does a counselling session take?

A complete counselling session including assessment and discussion typically takes around 60 to 90 minutes, depending on the plan and requirement.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a higher plan later, and the current plan fee can be adjusted toward the upgrade.

Are the counsellors qualified?

Yes. GCDA works with certified professionals experienced in career guidance, mentoring, and assessment-based counselling.

Do you offer online counselling?

Yes. Sessions can be delivered online as well as face-to-face, depending on the service and client preference.
